Custom Desk & Bookcase

A custom desk and integrated bookcase designed in Onshape and subsequently manufactured from panel-based furniture components. The project included the complete CAD layout, component sizing, connection planning, and dedicated drill guides for repeatable positioning of dowels and fasteners.

Project Overview

The objective was to design a large custom workstation and bookcase specifically for the available room space. The furniture was modelled as a complete assembly in Onshape before manufacture, allowing the overall proportions, storage layout, clearances, and component interfaces to be evaluated before material was cut.

The manufactured furniture closely follows the original CAD design. Some details, particularly the drawer construction, were modified during manufacture while retaining the overall layout and function.

Design Goals

  • Maximize usable desk and storage space within the available room.
  • Integrate the desk and bookcase into a single coordinated design.
  • Use readily manufacturable panel-based construction.
  • Design repeatable connections using dowels and furniture fasteners.
  • Create dedicated drill guides to accurately locate connection holes.
  • Validate dimensions and component relationships in CAD before manufacture.

From CAD to Finished Product

The final furniture was manufactured based on the Onshape design and maintains the principal geometry, shelving arrangement, workstation layout, and overall proportions of the CAD model.

The drawer implementation was adjusted during manufacture, demonstrating the normal design iteration that occurs when translating a digital model into a physical product.

Design & Final Result

CAD Development

The complete design was created in Onshape, allowing the individual components and their relationships to be developed and checked within the full furniture assembly.

Drill Guides

Dedicated drill guides were designed to improve repeatability when positioning the connection holes for dowels and fasteners. This reduces dependence on manual measurement during assembly.
