Custom Desk & Bookcase

A custom desk and bookcase designed in Onshape using practical furniture construction methods, with dedicated drill guides developed to accurately position dowels and fasteners.

Project Overview

This project involved the design of a custom desk and integrated bookcase intended for practical manufacture and assembly. The design was developed as a complete CAD model in Onshape.

Design Goals

  • Create a desk and bookcase suited to the available space.
  • Use practical furniture-style construction methods.
  • Make component positioning repeatable during assembly.
  • Develop dedicated drill guides for dowels and fasteners.
  • Keep the design suitable for real-world manufacture.

CAD Development

The complete design was created in Onshape, allowing the individual components and their relationships to be developed and checked within the full furniture assembly.

Drill Guides

Dedicated drill guides were designed to improve repeatability when positioning the connection holes for dowels and fasteners. This reduces dependence on manual measurement during assembly.
